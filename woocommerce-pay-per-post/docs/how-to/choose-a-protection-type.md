# Choose a protection type

Every protected post has one protection type. It decides what a purchase buys. This guide explains the four types and how to switch between them.

| Type | In the editor | What the customer gets | Version |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | **Buy once** | Buy once, read forever. | Free and Premium |
| Delay | **Free at first** | The post is free to everyone for a set time after you publish it. After that, readers must buy. | Premium |
| Page view | **Limited views** | A purchase allows a set number of views of the post. | Premium |
| Expiry | **Time limited** | A purchase gives access for a set time. | Premium |

Only one type applies to a post at a time.

## Set the type in the block editor

1. Open the post and the **Pay For Post** panel in the editor sidebar.
2. Choose the products under **Products that unlock it**.
3. Under **Protection**, choose **Buy once**, **Free at first**, **Limited views** or **Time limited**.
4. Fill in the settings that appear under the choices.
5. Save the post.

## Set the type in the classic meta box

1. Open the **Pay For Post** box below the editor.
2. Choose the products under **Products that unlock it**.
3. Under **Protection**, choose **Buy once**, **Free at first**, **Limited views** or **Time limited**.
4. Fill in the sentence that appears under the choices.
5. Update the post.

The line at the top of the panel or box says what a visitor will get, such as "Each purchase allows 3 views. Everyone else sees the paywall."

In the free version, the three Premium types are shown but locked, each with a **Premium** badge.

## Which one to use

- **Selling an article or download for good:** standard.
- **News that is free while it is fresh, then paid:** delay.
- **A post customers may read a few times, such as a printable pattern or a quiz:** page view.
- **A pass, such as 48 hours of video or a year of reports:** expiry.

## What all types share

- The customer must be logged in and have a Processing or Completed order for one of the post's products.
- Customers who get access through a membership or an active subscription are not limited by page views or expiry. Those limits apply to purchases.
- If a Premium licence lapses, delay, page view and expiry posts behave as standard posts until the licence is active again. Their settings are kept.

## Common pitfalls

- **More than one type stored.** The editor allows only one. If imported data holds more than one, delay wins, then page view, then expiry.
- **Changing type on a live post.** The new rule applies straight away to every customer, including those who bought under the old rule. A customer who bought standard access a year ago loses it if you switch the post to a 30-day expiry.
